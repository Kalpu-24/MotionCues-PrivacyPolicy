Privacy Policy for Motion Cues

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

Motion Cues ("the App"), developed by Kryoxx Studio ("we", "us", or "our"), is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use the Motion Cues mobile application.

Key Summary: Motion Cues processes motion, sensor, and vehicle recognition data 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share your personal data, sensor data, or activity recognition data with external servers or third parties.

1. Information We Access and Process Locally

To provide motion sickness mitigation features (visual cues overlay synchronized with vehicle motion), the App requires access to specific device hardware and APIs:

  • Sensors & Accelerometer Data: The App accesses device accelerometer and gyroscope sensors locally to calculate real-time tilt, acceleration, and motion vectors to render synchronized visual cues on screen.
  • Physical Activity Recognition (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`): The App uses Android's Activity Recognition API to automatically detect when you are in a moving vehicle (in-vehicle state) to trigger or pause the motion overlay. This data never leaves your device.
  • Display Over Other Apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`): The App uses overlay permissions strictly to render visual cue dots on top of other running applications while you are traveling.
  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used to display an active foreground service status notification allowing you to control or pause the overlay easily.

2. Data Collection, Storage, and Sharing

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location history, or device identifiers.
  • No External Data Transmission: All motion calculations and activity recognitions are computed in real-time in the device's volatile memory and are immediately discarded. Nothing is transmitted to external servers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics / Tracking: Motion Cues does not include third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics tools that track your usage across apps or websites.

3. In-App Purchases and Billing

Motion Cues uses the official Google Play Billing API to process optional Pro feature purchases. All financial transactions are handled directly and securely by Google Play. We do not store or have access to your credit card or financial details.

4. Data Security

Because all sensor and activity recognition processing is strictly confined to your local device, your motion and activity data remains protected within your device's Android security sandbox.

5. Children's Privacy

Motion Cues does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not collect personal data from any user.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
